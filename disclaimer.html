TwilightMenu Atelier LLC assumes zero legal liability for any adverse health outcomes, digestive discomfort, or metabolic irregularities arising from individual interpretations of our educational monographs or self-directed culinary experiments. While our sunset tasting courses are thoughtfully choreographed around principles of evening digestive ease, we make no clinical or therapeutic guarantees regarding enhanced sleep quality, hormonal balance, or metabolic longevity. Our culinary salon functions as an artistic and gastronomic sanctuary, not a clinical healthcare facility. Guests and readers remain solely responsible for evaluating their individual dietary suitability and consulting medical professionals regarding specific health concerns.

2. Consuming Raw or Undercooked Seafood & Meats Notice

In accordance with statutory mandates issued by the New York City Department of Health and Mental Hygiene and the United States Food and Drug Administration, we provide formal notice regarding raw and undercooked foodstuffs. Certain signature courses featured within our tasting progressions, including our citrus-cured sea crudo, lightly seared wild fish, and medium-rare hearth-roasted heritage beef, may contain raw or undercooked animal proteins. Consuming raw or undercooked meats, poultry, seafood, shellfish, or eggs may significantly elevate your risk of contracting foodborne illness, especially if you possess underlying medical conditions or compromised immune defenses. Guests who are pregnant, immunocompromised, or elderly are advised to request fully cooked alternatives for all animal protein courses.

Our culinary team adheres to rigorous food safety protocols, utilizing exclusively premium sushi-grade wild seafood, certified pathogen-free heritage livestock, and continuous temperature-monitored cold storage arrays. However, because natural culinary products are organic biological matter, zero-risk of foodborne pathogens cannot be mathematically guaranteed in raw culinary preparations. By electing to consume raw or lightly seared dishes during our tasting menus, guests knowingly acknowledge and accept these inherent biological risks. Our waitstaff is thoroughly trained to provide detailed ingredient provenance information and can facilitate fully cooked course substitutions whenever requested prior to service.

3. Natural Harvest Variance & Botanical Sourcing Realities

Our culinary philosophy is fundamentally rooted in hyper-seasonal, micro-batch sourcing from small organic family farms, wild botanical foragers, and regional day-boat fisheries. Consequently, agricultural yields, coastal weather conditions, and seasonal frost dates directly influence daily ingredient availability in our kitchen. Specific heirloom vegetable varieties, wild forest mushroom species, or cold-pressed botanical tinctures described on sample menus may be substituted with comparable seasonal elements without advance notice. These natural adaptations represent the authentic unpredictability of artisanal dining and will not be recognized as service failures or grounds for monetary refunds.

Furthermore, wild-foraged botanicals and seasonal mushrooms exhibit natural variations in earthy aroma, textural firmness, and bitterness profiles depending on regional soil composition and rainfall patterns. Our kitchen team rigorously inspects every wild-harvested element to ensure species authenticity and culinary safety before incorporating it into our sauces or infusions. Guests seeking completely standardized, chemically uniform, or mass-manufactured dining experiences are encouraged to consider conventional commercial restaurants rather than our living, season-driven culinary atelier. We take immense pride in celebrating nature's honest, ever-shifting palate at the evening table.

4. Zero-Proof Botanical Elixirs & Personal Tolerance

To provide sophisticated sensory pairings that elevate dining without relying on intoxicating spirits, TwilightMenu specializes in artisan zero-proof botanical elixirs, cold-extracted fruit verjus, and steeped mountain tisanes. These crafted elixirs incorporate diverse functional herbs, including bitter gentian root, chamomile blossoms, elderberry extracts, pine needle hydrosols, and green tea polyphenols. While formulated strictly from food-safe botanical ingredients, certain active herbal compounds may possess mild physiological properties that stimulate digestion or cause drowsiness. Guests who are pregnant, nursing, or taking prescription pharmaceuticals should review our detailed botanical ingredient lists with their server prior to consuming concentrated herbal pairings.

TwilightMenu disclaims legal liability for personal botanical sensitivities, mild allergic reactions to herbal tisanes, or interactions between functional plants and guest-prescribed medications. Our dining concierges maintain complete, itemized ingredient binders for every paired beverage and will happily arrange neutral mineral water or single-ingredient fruit pressings for guests who wish to avoid active botanicals. Guests remain solely accountable for monitoring their personal tolerance and communicating beverage preferences to their dedicated dining captain during dinner service.
